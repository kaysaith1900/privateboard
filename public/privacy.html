Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

PrivateBoard is a local-first application. Your boardroom sessions live on your own device, and we — the makers of PrivateBoard — operate no server that receives, stores, or sees your content. This policy explains exactly what stays on your device, what leaves it (and only to the providers you choose), and what we never collect.

01Who we are

“PrivateBoard” (also referred to as “the app”, “we”, “us”) is an application for iOS and desktop that convenes a panel of AI “directors” to discuss your questions. This policy applies to the PrivateBoard app on all platforms.

02The short version

  • We have no backend server. Your rooms, messages, agents, votes, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • There is no analytics SDK, no tracking, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service of any kind.
  • The app talks directly from your device to the AI, voice, and search providers you configure with your own API keys. Those providers process that content under their own privacy policies.

03Data stored on your device

Everything you create in PrivateBoard is written to a local database on your device (and on iOS, your API keys are stored in the system Keychain). This includes:

  • The questions you ask and the boardroom transcripts that result;
  • The AI directors you create or seat, and their configuration;
  • Round votes, key points, notes, and generated briefs/reports;
  • App preferences (theme, voice, delivery mode, etc.);
  • The API keys you enter for third-party providers.

This data never leaves your device except as described in Section 4. It is not backed up to any server we control. (It may be included in your own device/iCloud backups, which are governed by Apple’s policies and your settings.)

04Third-party providers you connect

PrivateBoard has no AI of its own. To generate discussion, synthesize voice, or search the web, the app sends the relevant content directly from your device to the provider(s) you have configured, authenticated with your own API key. We are not an intermediary — that traffic does not pass through any server of ours.

Depending on which features and providers you enable, the content sent may include your prompts, the discussion transcript, agent instructions, and (for voice) the text to be spoken or (for search) your search query. The providers and what they receive:

05What we do not collect

  • No accounts, sign-up, names, or email collection by us.
  • No usage analytics or telemetry sent to us or any third party.
  • No advertising identifiers and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location data.
  • No crash or diagnostic reporting service.
The “session analytics” summary shown for an adjourned room (turn counts, duration, vote tallies) is computed on your device from your own local data and shown only to you. It is never transmitted anywhere.

06App Store privacy label

Because PrivateBoard collects no data on the developer side, the app’s App Store privacy label is declared as “Data Not Collected.” Content you send to a third-party AI/voice/search provider is handled by that provider, not by us.

07Data retention & deletion

Because your data is local, you are in control of it:

  • Delete an individual room to remove its transcript and related data from your device.
  • Remove a stored API key in Settings to revoke the app’s access to that provider.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local database from your device.

To delete content you have already sent to a third-party provider, use that provider’s own controls or contact them directly.

08Security

API keys are stored in the operating system’s secure credential store (the iOS Keychain). All requests to third-party providers are made over encrypted HTTPS connections. As with any local data, the security of your device (passcode, disk encryption) protects your content.

09Children

PrivateBoard is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Since we collect no data at all, no such information is gathered.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
